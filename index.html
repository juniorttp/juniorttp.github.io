About Me

Hi, I'm Garry. For the last few years I've worked in B2B sales across HRIS, proptech and fintech. What I've always enjoyed most is the people side of the job. I like getting to know someone, working out what they actually need and helping the people around me get better at what they do.

Now I'm moving away from sales and building a career in web development. I'm teaching myself front end development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I built this site myself as part of that. Sales taught me to always think about the person on the other end, and that's something I want to bring into everything I build.

This is where I share what I've done so far and where I'm heading next. Thanks for stopping by.
